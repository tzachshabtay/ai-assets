AI Invaders Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

AI Invaders is a single-player arcade game. It does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information collection

AI Invaders does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party sign-in. The game does not collect gameplay activity, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Local settings

The game may save preferences such as master volume locally on your device. These settings remain on the device and are not sent to us or to a third party. You can remove them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

AI Invaders is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
